Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

This policy covers Kirian (formerly Kiran), a space shooter game by Oldřich Vořechovský / A+B — on Google Play as package com.ab.kiran, on the App Store as com.ol1n.kiran, and the desktop builds, including Steam. We do not collect personal information. To learn where the game is too hard or confusing, the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics, described below, which you can turn off at any time.

Anonymous Gameplay Statistics

What is sent. Short records of gameplay moments: the app was opened or went to the background, the main menu was ready (and how many milliseconds that took), a skin was chosen or bought (and how long it took to load), a sector started or ended (level, result, seconds flown, what caused a loss), and what happened in the ComCenter shop (time spent, number of purchases and upgrades). Each record also carries the app version, platform (for example iOS or Windows), app language, current skin, input type (touch, mouse, gamepad or keyboard), game mode and difficulty, and two coarse bands: roughly how many times the game has been played on this device (1, 2, 3–5, 6–10, 11+) and roughly how many days ago (0, 1, 2–6, 7–29, 30+). Credits and weapon power are sent as ranges, not exact values. The full list is published in the game's source repository (docs/telemetry.md).

Why. To see how many new players get from launch to their first mission, how long sessions last, whether the shop is understood and which levels are too hard, so we can fix them.

No identifiers. The statistics contain no name, no email, no pilot name or any other text you type, no account, no device or advertising identifier and no exact device model. Each play session gets a random number that exists only while the app runs, so two sessions cannot be linked to each other or to you. The statistics are not used for advertising or tracking and are not sold or shared.

Who receives them, and where. The statistics are received and stored by the developer, on the developer's own server in the EU (Czech Republic). Nobody else receives them: no analytics company or other third party is involved. Sending them, like any internet connection, reveals your IP address to that server; the IP address is not stored, it is used only to derive your country. Your country is the only location information kept.

How long. Statistics are kept for 12 months and then deleted.

Offline. The game works fully offline. Without a connection, up to 500 recent records wait on your device and are sent later; older ones are discarded.

How to turn them off. Pause the game and select USAGE STATS: ON so it reads USAGE STATS: OFF (the same menu as sound and music; the label is translated into your language). This takes effect immediately: records waiting on your device are deleted and nothing more is sent, until you turn it back on.

Data We Do Not Collect

Kirian does not collect:

— Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, account)

— Your pilot name or anything else you type

— Precise location (only the country, as above)

— Device identifiers, the advertising ID or the exact device model

— Contacts, photos, files or anything outside the game

Local Storage

Game progress (score, upgrades, settings, pilot name, purchased skins) is stored locally on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults). We never receive it. The statistics keep a few small values there — the day measuring began, a play-session counter and the last day band — from which the bands above are worked out on your device.

Local Co-op

Two-player co-op finds the other device on your local Wi-Fi network and exchanges game state with it directly. Nothing is sent to the internet or to us, and nothing is kept after the session.

Third-Party Services

Kirian contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The anonymous statistics go only to the developer's own server, as described above.

Leaderboards and achievements use Google Play Games Services on Android and Apple Game Center on iOS and macOS. If you are signed in to them, your score, deepest level and achievement progress are sent to Google or Apple under your player profile there. We do not receive that profile. See the Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases of skins are handled by Google Play Billing or the App Store. We receive no payment details.

The app uses Google Fonts to display its typefaces. On first launch it may download font files from Google's servers (fonts.gstatic.com); like any network request, this transmits your IP address to Google, but no personal data or identifiers are sent. Downloaded fonts are cached on your device and no further requests are made.

Children

Kirian is not directed at children under 13 and is not offered in the App Store Kids category or Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: the anonymous statistics described above contain nothing that identifies a person, and they can be turned off in the pause menu.
