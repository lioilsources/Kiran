Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Overview

This policy covers Kirian (formerly Kiran), a space shooter game by Oldřich Vořechovský / A+B — on Google Play as package com.ab.kiran, on the App Store as com.ol1n.kiran, and the desktop builds. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Data We Do Not Collect

Kirian does not collect:

— Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)

— Location data

— Device identifiers or the advertising ID

— Usage analytics or telemetry

Local Storage

Game progress (score, upgrades, settings, pilot name, purchased skins) is stored locally on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults). We never receive it.

Local Co-op

Two-player co-op finds the other device on your local Wi-Fi network and exchanges game state with it directly. Nothing is sent to the internet or to us, and nothing is kept after the session.

Third-Party Services

Kirian does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Leaderboards and achievements use Google Play Games Services on Android and Apple Game Center on iOS and macOS. If you are signed in to them, your score, deepest level and achievement progress are sent to Google or Apple under your player profile there. We do not receive that profile. See the Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases of skins are handled by Google Play Billing or the App Store. We receive no payment details.

The app uses Google Fonts to display its typefaces. On first launch it may download font files from Google's servers (fonts.gstatic.com); like any network request, this transmits your IP address to Google, but no personal data or identifiers are sent. Downloaded fonts are cached on your device and no further requests are made.

Children

Kirian is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app itself collects no data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
