Frequently Asked Questions

My game progress disappeared. Can it be restored?

Progress (high scores, settings, pilot name) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it; there is no cloud backup, so it cannot be restored remotely.

Does Kiran require an internet connection?

No. Kiran is fully playable offline. The app may download its display fonts from Google Fonts on first launch; everything else ships with the app.

Which platforms are supported?

iOS and Android, with desktop builds for macOS, Windows, and Linux available at olin.now.
